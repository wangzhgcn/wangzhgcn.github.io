--- layout: single author_profile: false classes: wide --- ## Research Interests My research focuses on mitochondrial molecular regulation and its roles in cellular aging, with particular emphasis on mitochondrial RNA modifications, gene expression, and mitochondrial homeostasis. ## Research Areas ### Mitochondrial RNA Modifications Investigating the regulation and biological functions of mitochondrial RNA modifications during aging. ### Mitochondrial Gene Expression Understanding how mitochondrial RNA processing and translation contribute to mitochondrial function. ### Cellular Aging Exploring the molecular mechanisms linking mitochondrial homeostasis to cellular senescence.
